How to Load & Test in Firefox

  1. Open Firefox and type about:debugging in the address bar.
  2. Click on "This Firefox" in the left sidebar.
  3. Click the "Load Temporary Add-on..." button.
  4. Navigate to your extension directory, open the firefox/ folder, and select the manifest.json file.
  5. Return to this tab, click any link above, and verify that tracking parameters are instantly stripped!
